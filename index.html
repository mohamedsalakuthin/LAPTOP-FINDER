Five things to check before you buy

RAM: 16 GB if you can

8 GB works for light use, but 16 GB keeps a laptop usable for years. Check whether it can be upgraded later.

Storage: SSD, 512 GB

Avoid hard disks. A 512 GB SSD gives room for apps and files without constant cleanup.

Screen: Full HD IPS

Look for 1920×1080 and good brightness (300 nits or more). Dim, washed-out panels tire your eyes.

Battery and weight

If you carry it daily, aim for under 1.8 kg and real-world battery of 6+ hours. Read independent reviews, not just claims.

Service and warranty

Check that the brand has a service centre in your city and whether accidental-damage cover is available.

These are model families, not exact listings. Specs and prices change often, so compare current prices on Amazon, Flipkart and brand stores, and read recent reviews before buying.
